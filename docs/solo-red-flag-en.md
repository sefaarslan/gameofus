# Red Flag Mayın Tarlası — Senaryolar (EN)

60 senaryo. Doğru cevap yoktur; skor yalnızca Green/Yellow/Red dağılımıdır.

## Sınırlar  `boundaries`

1. Your partner still keeps a gift from their ex on a shelf in their room.
2. When you make plans with friends, your partner says “Of course, have fun” and genuinely means it.
3. When your friend is wiped out, they say “I can't talk today, can I call you tomorrow?”
4. Someone you just met keeps texting “Where are you, who are you with?” all through the first week.
5. Your partner went through your phone without asking, saying “I was just curious.”
6. A friend laughingly told a group something private about you and said “I was only joking.”
7. When you said you wanted a weekend to yourself, your partner said “Okay, take care of yourself.”
8. Your partner ordered for you at a restaurant without asking and said “Don't eat that.”
9. A friend brought guests to your home without warning and said “That's fine, right?”
10. In the first month, your partner suggested you share all your social media passwords.

## Güven  `trust`

1. A friend told you a small lie to hide a surprise birthday plan.
2. Your partner answered “Where are you?” three hours later with “My phone died.”
3. Your partner texts their ex once a week and tells you about it openly.
4. A friend shared your secret with someone else, saying “I only told my closest friend.”
5. Your partner revealed a long-hidden debt in the middle of an argument.
6. A friend who often forgets promises apologized with “I forgot, I'm sorry, let me make it up to you.”
7. Your partner never shows you their bank statements but pays their share of joint costs on time every month.
8. A friend speaks well of you in front of others but criticizes you in private.
9. When you ask who they're with late at night, your partner pauses, says “A friend,” and changes the subject.
10. A friend deleted a private message of yours that reached them by mistake, without reading it, and let you know.

## İletişim  `communication`

1. During an argument, your partner says “I'm getting too worked up, can we cool off for half an hour and talk?”
2. When something hurts them, your friend goes quiet for days and only says “I'm fine.”
3. Your partner makes a big decision, like moving, without telling you, then says “I was going to tell you.”
4. In every argument, your friend starts sentences with “You're always like this.”
5. Your partner waits for a calm evening to tell you upsetting news instead of dropping it on you at once.
6. Your friend doesn't answer your messages for days but is active on social media.
7. When you want to talk about a problem, your partner puts the phone down and gives you their full attention.
8. Two weeks after an argument, your partner suddenly brings it up again, saying “It's still on my mind.”
9. When you share your view, your friend shuts it down with “You're overreacting.”
10. Even when they disagree with you, your partner explains their view calmly and respectfully.

## Kıskançlık / Sahiplenme  `jealousy`

1. Your partner asks “Who's that?” about someone in a photo of you with friends.
2. When you get closer to someone else, your friend sulks, saying “You don't like me anymore.”
3. Your partner says they'd rather not join your dinner with a coworker, but tells you “Have a good time.”
4. Your partner checks the posts you like on social media one by one and asks about them.
5. Your partner asks about your ex and adds “I was curious, but we don't have to talk about it.”
6. When you get a promotion, your friend greets it coldly with “So you're just that lucky.”
7. Your partner bombards you with questions about what you talked about with friends while they weren't there.
8. Your partner eagerly listens about an event you went to without them and says “I'm so glad, tell me everything.”
9. When you spend time with another friend, your friend messages you “You left me out.”
10. Your partner compares you to others, saying “Other people are so much more attentive.”

## Para & Yaşam Tarzı  `money_lifestyle`

1. Your partner suggests saving monthly for a shared goal and sets aside their own share in advance.
2. At every meal, your friend leaves you the bill, saying “We'll even it out later.”
3. When you ask what they do with their salary, your partner shuts it down with “That's my business.”
4. On your birthday, your partner buys an expensive gift that stretches your budget, saying “You deserve it.”
5. A friend returns money they borrowed and adds a small gift as thanks.
6. Your partner plans the whole vacation alone and only tells you the dates.
7. Your partner keeps a chart of household chores and nudges you to split them fifty-fifty.
8. Your friend cancels plans at the last minute again and again, saying “I have no energy.”
9. Your partner supports you moving cities for a career opportunity and suggests trying long distance.
10. Your partner made a large purchase from your joint account without telling you.

## Saygı  `respect`

1. In front of others, your friend cuts you off and brushes off your opinion with “Don't be silly.”
2. When your partner gets something wrong, they say right away “You're right, I'm sorry.”
3. Your partner keeps commenting “You'd look better if you changed your outfit.”
4. While you talk, your friend keeps checking their phone but says “I'm listening.”
5. Your partner tells their friends funny but hurtful stories about you.
6. In an argument, your partner leaves the room without raising their voice, saying “Let's talk about this later.”
7. While congratulating you on an achievement, your friend adds “But this part was missing.”
8. When you say “No,” your partner doesn't push, but acts distant for a while.
9. When arguing with you, your friend lists your past mistakes one by one.
10. In front of others, your partner laughs and says “You don't know anything anyway.”
