# Red Flag Mayın Tarlası — Setler (EN)

54 aktif senaryo, 6 set × 9 kart. Doğru cevap yoktur; skor yalnızca Green/Yellow/Red dağılımıdır. Yüzdeler yalnızca aynı set içinde karşılaştırılabilir. 104-106 setleri mobilde açılacaktır (henüz içerik yok).

## Arkadaşlık · 101  `friend-101`

1. A friend returns money they borrowed and adds a small gift as thanks.  _(Para & Yaşam Tarzı · sağlıklı)_
2. When you share your view, your friend shuts it down with “You're overreacting.”  _(İletişim · endişe verici)_
3. A friend told you a small lie to hide a surprise birthday plan.  _(Güven · sağlıklı)_
4. When you tell your friend you're going on holiday with someone else, they say “Okay, have fun” and don't text for a few days.  _(Kıskançlık / Sahiplenme · gri)_
5. When your friend is wiped out, they say “I can't talk today, can I call you tomorrow?”  _(Sınırlar · sağlıklı)_
6. While congratulating you on an achievement, your friend adds “But this part was missing.”  _(Saygı · gri)_
7. A friend speaks well of you in front of others but criticizes you in private.  _(Güven · endişe verici)_
8. While you talk, your friend keeps checking their phone but says “I'm listening.”  _(Saygı · gri)_
9. A friend laughingly told a group something private about you and said “I was only joking.”  _(Sınırlar · endişe verici)_

## Arkadaşlık · 102  `friend-102`

1. When you can't reply right away, your friend doesn't push and says “Write back when you're free.”  _(Sınırlar · sağlıklı)_
2. A friend shared your secret with someone else, saying “I only told my closest friend.”  _(Güven · endişe verici)_
3. In front of others, your friend cuts you off and brushes off your opinion with “Don't be silly.”  _(Saygı · endişe verici)_
4. A friend who often forgets promises apologized with “I forgot, I'm sorry, let me make it up to you.”  _(Güven · gri)_
5. After a shared expense, your friend works out the split right away and texts you a clear “My share is this much.”  _(Para & Yaşam Tarzı · sağlıklı)_
6. When you join a new group of friends, your friend says “I'm so happy for you, introduce us sometime.”  _(Kıskançlık / Sahiplenme · sağlıklı)_
7. At every meal, your friend leaves you the bill, saying “We'll even it out later.”  _(Para & Yaşam Tarzı · gri)_
8. Your friend doesn't answer your messages for days but is active on social media.  _(İletişim · gri)_
9. When you get a promotion, your friend greets it coldly with “So you're just that lucky.”  _(Kıskançlık / Sahiplenme · endişe verici)_

## Arkadaşlık · 103  `friend-103`

1. When a joke of theirs makes you uncomfortable, your friend apologizes right away and doesn't repeat it.  _(Sınırlar · sağlıklı)_
2. When arguing with you, your friend lists your past mistakes one by one.  _(Saygı · endişe verici)_
3. When something hurts them, your friend goes quiet for days and only says “I'm fine.”  _(İletişim · endişe verici)_
4. A friend brought guests to your home without warning and said “That's fine, right?”  _(Sınırlar · gri)_
5. When you spend time with another friend, your friend messages you “You left me out.”  _(Kıskançlık / Sahiplenme · gri)_
6. In every argument, your friend starts sentences with “You're always like this.”  _(İletişim · endişe verici)_
7. A friend deleted a private message of yours that reached them by mistake, without reading it, and let you know.  _(Güven · sağlıklı)_
8. Your friend cancels plans at the last minute again and again, saying “I have no energy.”  _(Para & Yaşam Tarzı · gri)_
9. When you get closer to someone else, your friend sulks, saying “You don't like me anymore.”  _(Kıskançlık / Sahiplenme · endişe verici)_

## Sevgili · 101  `romantic-101`

1. During an argument, your partner says “I'm getting too worked up, can we cool off for half an hour and talk?”  _(İletişim · sağlıklı)_
2. Your partner never shows you their bank statements but pays their share of joint costs on time every month.  _(Güven · gri)_
3. Your partner asks “Who's that?” about someone in a photo of you with friends.  _(Kıskançlık / Sahiplenme · gri)_
4. Your partner went through your phone without asking, saying “I was just curious.”  _(Sınırlar · endişe verici)_
5. Your partner revealed a long-hidden debt in the middle of an argument.  _(Güven · endişe verici)_
6. When your partner gets something wrong, they say right away “You're right, I'm sorry.”  _(Saygı · sağlıklı)_
7. Your partner asks about your ex and adds “I was curious, but we don't have to talk about it.”  _(Kıskançlık / Sahiplenme · sağlıklı)_
8. In the first month, your partner suggested you share all your social media passwords.  _(Sınırlar · endişe verici)_
9. Your partner plans the whole vacation alone and only tells you the dates.  _(Para & Yaşam Tarzı · endişe verici)_

## Sevgili · 102  `romantic-102`

1. Your partner eagerly listens about an event you went to without them and says “I'm so glad, tell me everything.”  _(Kıskançlık / Sahiplenme · sağlıklı)_
2. Your partner makes a big decision, like moving, without telling you, then says “I was going to tell you.”  _(İletişim · endişe verici)_
3. When you say “No,” your partner doesn't push, but acts distant for a while.  _(Saygı · gri)_
4. Your partner made a large purchase from your joint account without telling you.  _(Para & Yaşam Tarzı · endişe verici)_
5. Two weeks after an argument, your partner suddenly brings it up again, saying “It's still on my mind.”  _(İletişim · gri)_
6. Your partner keeps commenting “You'd look better if you changed your outfit.”  _(Saygı · endişe verici)_
7. Your partner answered “Where are you?” three hours later with “My phone died.”  _(Güven · gri)_
8. When you said you wanted a weekend to yourself, your partner said “Okay, take care of yourself.”  _(Sınırlar · sağlıklı)_
9. Your partner bombards you with questions about what you talked about with friends while they weren't there.  _(Kıskançlık / Sahiplenme · endişe verici)_

## Sevgili · 103  `romantic-103`

1. Your partner supports you moving cities for a career opportunity and suggests trying long distance.  _(Para & Yaşam Tarzı · sağlıklı)_
2. Your partner still keeps a gift from their ex on a shelf in their room.  _(Sınırlar · gri)_
3. Your partner checks the posts you like on social media one by one and asks about them.  _(Kıskançlık / Sahiplenme · endişe verici)_
4. When you make plans with friends, your partner says “Of course, have fun” and genuinely means it.  _(Sınırlar · sağlıklı)_
5. Your partner tells their friends funny but hurtful stories about you.  _(Saygı · endişe verici)_
6. Your partner waits for a calm evening to tell you upsetting news instead of dropping it on you at once.  _(İletişim · sağlıklı)_
7. Your partner texts their ex once a week and tells you about it openly.  _(Güven · gri)_
8. On your birthday, your partner buys an expensive gift that stretches your budget, saying “You deserve it.”  _(Para & Yaşam Tarzı · gri)_
9. When you ask who they're with late at night, your partner pauses, says “A friend,” and changes the subject.  _(Güven · endişe verici)_
